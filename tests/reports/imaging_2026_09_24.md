# Physics-based room imaging without ML (Phase 6: 6.1, 6.2, 6.4, 6.5) — 2026-09-24

**Headline.** Sound does reveal room geometry. On the v2 held-out
archive, every classical imager fused with the no-audio prior beats the
prior. The margin is many standard errors, and the imagers were tuned on
training rooms only. The best closed-form method gives held-out IoU
**0.189 vs 0.101** (paired ΔIoU **+0.088 ± 0.005, z = 19.3**; 500 rooms,
K = 4 poses). This method, "all physics", is the prior logit plus four
standardised physics images, with five weights fitted by logistic
regression on training rooms. The simplest candidate (prior +
a·back-projection + b) reaches 0.168 (z = 16.8). With all 8 poses the
fusion reaches 0.244 (z = 34.1). Full-waveform inversion, run on 40 rooms
as a proof of concept, reaches IoU 0.337 against 0.087 for the prior on
the same rooms (z = 4.5). **The Phase 6 "done when" criterion is met.**
The published Phase 2 CNNs (0.100) sit at the prior. The information was
in the recordings, and the CNNs did not extract it.

Caveats that bound this claim are listed in [Limitations](#limitations).
The most important one: the data are noise-free simulations, and the
imagers know the exact poses and the empty outer box. FWI inverts with
the same simulator that generated the data (an "inverse crime").

## Protocol (no held-out tuning)

- **Data.** Train `active_sensing_v2_train_10kx4.hdf5`, held-out
  `active_sensing_v2_heldout_500x8.hdf5`: 64² grid, p = 0 walls and
  obstacles, 2 mics 12 cells apart, chirp 0.02 → 0.45, 400 steps.
  `ir.source_drive` reproduces the stored recordings bit for bit.
- **Priors.** The filled-mask prior $\hat\pi$ is the mean over training
  rooms 500-9999 (9500 rooms, mean 0.0696). The illuminated-boundary
  prior is the mean illuminated mask of training rooms 500-3499 with their
  own 4 poses (mean 0.0169).
- **Development sweep** (training rooms 0-199, split in half) chose the
  imager settings: back-projection gate, envelope filter, no spreading
  weight, carving onset detector, and the FWI step size and TV weight (3
  training rooms). See `docs/imaging.md` §6.
- **Fit.** Training rooms 0-499 (K = 4). Per method, the smoothing
  σ ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3} (and, for carving, the onset detector) is chosen by
  training log-loss. The fusion $\operatorname{logit}q =
  \operatorname{logit}\hat\pi + \sum a_i z_i + b$ is fitted by logistic
  regression. The IoU threshold τ maximises the training mean IoU.
- **Score.** All 500 held-out rooms, first K = 4 poses (the training pose
  count), unless stated. Per room: IoU at τ, average precision, boundary
  F (1-cell tolerance) at τ, and information gain over the prior (bits).
  Every method is compared to the prior with the paired per-room
  difference: mean ± SE, and z = mean/SE.
- The held-out archive was read before scoring only twice, never to tune
  anything: a bit-exactness check of one recording and a timing check of
  the target code.

Reading these rows:

- **Pose count.** The imagers keep improving from 4 to 8 poses (0.189 →
  0.244), unlike the CNN fusion, which saturated at K ≈ 4.
- **Noise.** At 20 dB SNR per recording, back-projection loses almost
  nothing. The raw-residual onset detector used for carving fails under
  noise. The training-side selection switched carving to the
  matched-filter envelope at a 0.2 relative threshold, which keeps most
  of its gain.
- **FWI.** It runs 50 Adam iterations over three frequency stages, about
  9 s per room on one core. The mean final misfit is 0.074 of the
  empty-room misfit. FWI recovers thin walls almost exactly and traces
  the illuminated surfaces of solid objects (`fwi.png`), with a boundary
  F of 0.64-0.67 against 0.16-0.18 for the prior. Its occupancy is not
  calibrated (information gain of FWI alone: −1010 bits/room). Fusing
  with the prior fixes that (+146 bits).

### Standalone images (no prior), AP on the filled mask

Back-projection 0.194, echo ellipses 0.132, time reversal 0.130,
carving 0.112, against 0.128 for the prior's ranking. Carving is purely
negative evidence: it knows where obstacles are *not*, so it needs the
prior to rank the rest.

### Findings along the way

- **Background subtraction is the key step.** The scattered residual
  $y - y_\text{empty}$ is exactly zero until the first scattered wave
  arrives. Its onset alone gives the carving image, the second-strongest
  single method.
- **Time reversal is exact but weak here.** Re-emitting reversed
  residuals equals the autograd FWI gradient at o = 0 to 1e-5 (a unit
  test), but the fitted fusion weight is *negative*: the smoothed image
  is anti-correlated with occupancy. The p = 0 obstacles are far outside
  the Born regime (the residual energy often exceeds the recording's),
  and the zero-lag image is dominated by low-wavenumber transmission
  correlations rather than reflector sharpness. Iterating past the
  linearisation (FWI) is what pays.
- **Spreading compensation hurts** delay-and-sum (the $\sqrt{r_s r_m}$
  weight amplifies the late, multiply scattered tail). So does gating
  too early (120 lags). Tikhonov and spectral Wiener are equivalent here
  (development AP 0.2750 vs 0.2749).

## 6.4 What can a laptop hear? CRLB design chart

Single planar reflector, known chirp, white noise, SNR = matched-filter
output SNR (20 dB here). The envelope bound uses $\beta = B/\sqrt{12}$.
Bearing is broadside, from TDOA across the array, with range as a
nuisance parameter. SI units, `LAPTOP_ROOM` scale (c = 343 m/s).

| setup | range σ (envelope) | range σ (coherent) | bearing σ | lateral error at 3 m | range resolution c/2B | feature λmin/2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LAPTOP_ROOM sim band 0.3-1.7 kHz, 2 mics 20 cm | 4.8 mm | 1.8 mm | 5.5° | 29 cm | 12.2 cm | 10.1 cm |
| laptop 0.3-8 kHz, 2 mics 20 cm | 0.87 mm | 0.41 mm | 0.99° | 5.2 cm | 2.2 cm | 2.1 cm |
| same, 8 poses | 0.31 mm | 0.15 mm | 0.35° | 1.8 cm | 2.2 cm | 2.1 cm |
| laptop 4-mic line array, 7 cm pitch | 0.61 mm | 0.29 mm | 0.90° | 4.7 cm | 2.2 cm | 2.1 cm |
| near-ultrasound 17-21 kHz, 2 mics | 1.7 mm | 0.10 mm | 1.9° | 10 cm | 4.3 cm | 0.8 cm |
| phone 0.1-20 kHz, 2 mics 15 cm | 0.34 mm | 0.17 mm | 0.51° | 2.7 cm | 0.9 cm | 0.9 cm |

Reading: range to one wall is never the bottleneck. Sub-millimetre to
millimetre bounds hold at 20 dB for any audible band. What limits a
laptop is **resolution**: separating reflectors needs Δ ≥ c/2B, and
resolving features needs λmin/2. Two 20 cm mics give **bearing** of
0.5-5°, i.e. 3-30 cm laterally at 3 m. Every setup has mic spacing above
λmin/2, so coherent (phase) bearing is ambiguous (grating lobes). Only
the envelope TDOA bound is safe. More poses help as 1/√K, which matches
the K = 4 → 8 gain above.

The v2 grid protocol, read at 2.5 cm cells, is a 1.6 m room sounded at
0.27-6.2 kHz with a 30 cm baseline. In grid units its envelope range
bound at 20 dB is 0.023 cells, against a resolution of 1.16 cells. The
measured imaging error is set by resolution and multipath, not by
noise.

## 6.5 Room parameters

The drive is a DC-free chirp, the second difference of a tapered chirp
(see `docs/imaging.md`: a soft source with non-zero mean pumps a
quasi-static mode). The room is 160 × 110 cells with impedance walls of
admittance β and 14 rigid scatterers. There are 3 mics, 18 000 steps,
Wiener deconvolution band-limited to 0.04-0.2, and a T20 fit.

| β | α (2D diffuse, truth) | T60 estimated (±sd over mics) | Eyring prediction | Sabine prediction | α from Eyring inversion | α from Sabine inversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.05 | 0.250 | 5610 ± 73 | 4912 | 5653 | 0.223 | 0.252 |
| 0.1 | 0.427 | 3026 ± 106 | 2542 | 3314 | 0.374 | 0.468 |
| 0.2 | 0.659 | 1733 ± 29 | 1315 | 2147 | 0.558 | 0.817 |
| 0.4 | 0.879 | 1092 ± 28 | 671 | 1610 | 0.726 | 1.296 |

The estimated T60 always lies between the Eyring and Sabine predictions,
as the Phase 5 verification found with a Ricker source (5512, 2996 and
1629 for β = 0.05, 0.1 and 0.2). The spread over mics is 1-4 %. Eyring
inversion therefore underestimates the mean absorption by 11-17 %, and
Sabine inversion overestimates it once α > 0.4. This is a bias of the
diffuse-field model in a small 2D room, not of the estimator. The DRR ranges
from −1.2 dB (β = 0.4) to −10.7 dB (β = 0.05). It grows with β and is
generally highest at the mic nearest the source. The v2 archive rooms are lossless (p = 0 everywhere, 400
steps), so T60 is undefined there. Room parameters are validated on
absorbing rooms only.

## Limitations

1. **Noise-free simulation, exact poses.** The 20 dB noise row shows
   the gain survives additive white noise. It does not model pose error
   (plan 6.6.1), device responses, or 3D propagation.
2. **Known background.** The imagers subtract the engine's response of
   the empty outer box, which is identical in every archive room. In a
   real room the walls are part of the unknown. There, the analogue is a
   background measured or estimated beforehand, or treating the walls as
   targets (FWI does not need the subtraction).
3. **Inverse crime in FWI.** FWI inverts with the exact simulator and
   grid that produced the data, so 0.337 is an upper-end number. It ran
   on 40 rooms with 12 training rooms for its threshold and fusion.
   Treat it as a proof of concept, as planned.
4. **Illuminated-boundary target.** It is geometric: straight rays,
   first-order outer-wall bounces, no diffraction or obstacle-to-obstacle
   paths. The held-out target uses the same 4 poses as the images.
5. **Multiple comparisons.** 6 methods × 2 targets × 3 metrics. Every
   headline z is ≥ 9, so no correction changes a conclusion. The
   no-audio control shows how small an effect can still be "significant"
   (+0.0004 IoU at z = 4.5). Effect sizes are what matter.
